# Mini Remote: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

Mini Remote is a TV remote control app. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer receives none of your data.

## What the app stores, on your phone only
- The TVs you add: name, brand, local network (IP) address. Each TV's pairing token and security-certificate fingerprint are encrypted with a key held in the phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore).
- An Android / Google TV pairing identity (a private key that stays inside the Android Keystore).
- Your quick access items, TV channels, added apps and settings.
- Cached files: a downloaded app update, and app logos (when enabled).

Nothing is backed up to the cloud or copied to a new phone. Use **Settings › Privacy & data › Erase all data** to delete it all, or uninstall the app.

## Network connections
- **Your TVs**, on your home Wi-Fi only. The app refuses public internet addresses for TV control and scans only your phone's local Wi-Fi subnet when you search for TVs.
- **GitHub** (github.com), to check for and download app updates, when Automatic updates is on.
- **Simple Icons** (cdn.simpleicons.org), the **apps' own websites** (their app icon, e.g. netflix.com/apple-touch-icon.png) and **Google's favicon service** (www.google.com/s2/favicons), to download app logos, when App logos is on. Only the app's name or website (for example "netflix" or "hotstar.com") is requested.

## Voice search
The microphone is used only while you tap the mic in the Search panel, and it stops when you finish speaking or tap it again. Android's speech recognizer turns speech into text, on the phone where possible (otherwise through your phone's speech service, for example Google's). Mini Remote receives only the text and types it on your TV. No audio is saved.

## Permissions
- **Internet, network state, Wi-Fi state:** find and control TVs on your network; check for updates.
- **Microphone:** voice search, only while listening.
- **Vibrate:** haptic feedback.
- **Post notifications:** tell you when an update is ready.
- **Request install packages:** hand a downloaded update to Android's installer. You always confirm.

## Children
The app isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
